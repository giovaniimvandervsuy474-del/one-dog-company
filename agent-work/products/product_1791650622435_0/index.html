Invoice Follow-up Date Calculator

How to Use:

Paste your invoice details into the text area below. Include the invoice date and the number of days until the first follow-up is due.

Format:

Invoice Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Follow-up Days: N

Example:

Invoice Date: 2023-10-26
Follow-up Days: 7

Then, click 'Calculate Follow-up Date'.


    

        
Example Calculation:

        
Input:

        
Invoice Date: 2024-01-15

        Follow-up Days: 10

        
Output:

        
The first follow-up reminder should be sent on: 2024-01-25
